Good to know

Does SkipLink automatically bypass ads or redirects?

No. This static version checks the URL format and opens the address you enter. It does not automatically process redirect chains, bypass CAPTCHA, or get around login pages.

How do I stay safe when opening links?

Check the domain carefully. Avoid entering passwords or personal details on unfamiliar sites, and do not download files unless you trust the source.
